# Important Files Analysis

## Verilog (.v)

Defines the digital logic of the design.

---

## LEF (.lef)

Provides the physical abstract information of the analog macro, including dimensions, pins, and routing blockages.

---

## LIB (.lib)

Contains timing and power models used during timing analysis.

---

## GDS (.gds)

Contains the complete physical layout of the analog macro.

---

## Configuration Files

Control OpenLane execution, including synthesis, floorplanning, placement, routing, and power planning.

---

## Learning Outcome

Each file serves a unique purpose in the physical design flow. Together, they enable OpenLane to correctly integrate the analog macro into the digital implementation.
